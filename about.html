About Offscript

Offscript is a small weekend-planning project for people who want an easy nudge to explore their city.

Our idea

Make it easier to find a nearby walk, a game with friends, a café catch-up, or a new view over the lake. Start with simple filters and useful map links.

Our approach

Keep listings tied to sources, label nearby day trips, and avoid promising current opening hours or prices without confirmation. Check details with the venue before setting out.

Current coverage: Bhopal has the first city directory. Other city pages link to Maps until their place listings are sourced.
