How Offscript works

Start with a city, find an idea that fits the day, and check the details before you go.

1

Choose your city

Type a city or choose a popular destination. Bhopal is the first city with a sourced place directory.

2

Filter the kind of outing

Browse by nature, sports, cafés, sunset, heritage, time, and companion group.

3

Check the place details

Open the map search and source link. Confirm current access, prices, timings, and bookings before travelling.

Why a source link? Place access and costs can change. Offscript shares where a listing came from and leaves the latest visit checks to the linked venue, authority, or map listing.
